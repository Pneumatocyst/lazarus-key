# Security and responsible use

Lazarus Key is intended for maintenance of computers that you own or are explicitly authorized to support.

## Reporting a project issue

Do not open a public issue containing passwords, recovery keys, private IP addresses, serial numbers, patient information, customer data, or diagnostic reports. Describe the behavior with sanitized sample data.

## Operational guidance

- Verify ISO checksums before booting them.
- Prefer offline use for aging recovery environments.
- Treat recovered files and generated reports as sensitive.
- Store BitLocker recovery keys outside the Lazarus Key.
- Do not commit third-party binaries, ISO images, reports, or recovered data.
- Review every destructive disk operation before confirmation.

The project launcher itself performs read-only collection. Third-party recovery environments may contain destructive capabilities and remain subject to their own documentation and licenses.
